Lesson 2

Interface & Navigation

Get comfortable inside Adobe Photoshop. Learn how to move around the workspace, manage panels, and save your projects with ease.

The Photoshop Workspace

When you first open Photoshop, you'll see a layout made up of several key areas: the Menu Bar at the top, the Options Bar just below it, the Toolbox on the left, and various Panels on the right. In the center is your Canvas — where all your creative work happens.

Understanding where everything lives is the first step to working efficiently in Photoshop.

Key Interface Areas

  • Menu Bar — Access all commands like File, Edit, Image, Layer, and more
  • Options Bar — Shows settings for whichever tool is currently selected
  • Toolbox — Your main toolkit: selection, painting, retouching, and more
  • Panels — Layers, Properties, History, Color, and other controls
  • Canvas — Your working area where the image is displayed
💡

Pro tip: You can reset your workspace to the default layout anytime via Window → Workspace → Reset Essentials.

Navigating the Canvas

Use the Hand Tool (H) to pan around your canvas, and the Zoom Tool (Z) to zoom in or out. You can also hold Alt/Option + scroll wheel to zoom quickly without switching tools. To fit the entire image in view, press Ctrl+0 (Cmd+0 on Mac).

Saving Your Work

Photoshop uses its own file format, .PSD, which preserves all your layers and edits. Always save in PSD while working. When you're ready to export, use File → Export → Export As to save as JPG, PNG, or another web-friendly format.

⚠️

Important: Save your work frequently with Ctrl+S (Cmd+S). Photoshop does not auto-save by default.
