Colors & Brushes

Bring your ideas to life with colors and custom brushes. Control size, opacity, and flow for better creativity.

Foreground & Background Colors

At the bottom of the Toolbox, you'll see two overlapping squares — these represent your Foreground and Background colors. The foreground color is what your Brush and other painting tools use. Click either square to open the Color Picker and choose any color.

Press D to reset to default black and white, and X to swap foreground and background colors.

Using the Color Picker

The Color Picker lets you choose colors by clicking on the spectrum, entering a HEX code, or adjusting HSB/RGB sliders. For consistent branding, entering a HEX value is the most precise method.

💡

Tip: Use the Eyedropper Tool (I) to sample any color directly from your canvas or even from anywhere on your screen.

The Brush Tool

Press B to activate the Brush Tool. In the Options Bar you can control:

  • Size — How large the brush tip is (use [ and ] keys to resize quickly)
  • Hardness — How sharp or soft the brush edges are
  • Opacity — How transparent each brush stroke is
  • Flow — How quickly paint builds up as you paint

Brush Presets

Photoshop comes with dozens of built-in brush presets — from basic round brushes to textured and splatter brushes. Open the Brush Preset Picker by clicking the brush icon in the Options Bar. You can also download and install custom brushes from the internet.

⚠️

Best practice: Always paint on a separate layer rather than directly on your image, so you can adjust or remove your strokes without affecting the original.
