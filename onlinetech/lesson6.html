Selections & Masks

Edit smarter by selecting specific areas and using masks to hide or reveal parts without deleting anything.

Why Selections Matter

A selection lets you isolate a specific part of your image so that any edits only apply to that area. Without selections, every change you make affects the entire layer. Mastering selections is key to precise, professional editing.

Selection Tools

  • Rectangular & Elliptical Marquee (M) — Select geometric shapes
  • Lasso Tool (L) — Freehand selection by drawing around an object
  • Polygonal Lasso — Click to create straight-edged selections
  • Magic Wand (W) — Selects areas of similar color with one click
  • Quick Selection (W) — Brush over an area to intelligently select it
  • Object Select Tool — AI-powered selection of entire objects
💡

Tip: After making a selection, go to Select → Modify → Feather to soften the edges for a more natural look.

What Are Layer Masks?

A Layer Mask is a grayscale image attached to a layer that controls its visibility. White areas on the mask reveal the layer, black areas hide it, and gray areas make it partially transparent. The key advantage: nothing is permanently deleted — you can always paint back what you hid.

Adding a Layer Mask

Select the layer you want to mask, then click the Add Layer Mask button (rectangle with a circle) at the bottom of the Layers Panel. Paint on the mask with a black brush to hide parts of the layer, or white to reveal them. Press Alt/Option + click the mask thumbnail to view the mask directly.

⚠️

Non-destructive rule: Always use Layer Masks instead of the Eraser Tool. Masks let you undo any hiding at any time.
